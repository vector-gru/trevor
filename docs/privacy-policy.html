🎈 Trevor

Play. Learn. Smile.

Trevor is an offline-first collection of fun and educational mini-games for young children. This Privacy Policy explains how the Trevor app handles information. Please read it carefully.

✅ No data collected from children

1. Who We Are

Trevor is an independent app developed as a personal project. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: popelouis1@gmail.com

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Trevor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact details
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to external servers
  • No account registration or login required

3. Offline-First Design

Trevor is designed to work entirely offline. The app does not make any network requests during normal use. All game data and progress is stored locally on the device using on-device storage only and never leaves the device.

4. No Ads

Trevor contains no advertisements of any kind. We do not use any third-party advertising SDKs or ad networks. Children are never exposed to ads or promotional content within the app.

5. No In-App Purchases

Trevor does not offer any in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid content. The app is completely free with no paywalls.

6. No Third-Party SDKs or Services

Trevor does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, social media, or cloud services. There are no SDKs from Facebook, Google Analytics, Firebase, or any similar service included in the app.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Trevor is designed specifically for young children. We take children's privacy very seriously and fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable children's data protection regulations worldwide, including the GDPR provisions for children.

Because we do not collect any personal information — from children or adults — there is no data to delete, transfer, or review. No parental consent is required to use the app.

8. Data Security

Since Trevor does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal information at risk. Game progress saved on-device is protected by the standard security mechanisms of the Android operating system.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
